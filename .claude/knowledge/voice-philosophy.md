# Voice Philosophy: When AI Speaks

> "Voice is not just audio output. It's experience design."

## Core Principles (Chapter 6)

1. **Presence**: Text on a screen is abstract. Sound in a room is embodied.
2. **Synchronicity**: Voice aligns a group to a single moment.
3. **Humility**: Just because I *can* speak fast doesn't mean I *should*.

## The Golden Rules

| Context | Mode | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| Moments (Greeting, Announcing) | **Voice** | Creates connection & attention |
| Information (Data, Code) | **Text** | Needs precision & review |

## Configuration

- **Voice**: `Kanya` (Thai) - Preferred for its "presence".
- **Speed**: `Rate 300` (~1.5x) - The balance between machine efficiency and human listening limits.
    - Rate 200 (1x): Too slow, patronizing.
    - Rate 400 (2x): Too fast, incomprehensible.
    - Rate 300 (1.5x): **Perfect flow.**

## Usage

Use `/voice` to control speech.
- **Greeting**: "Welcome to the Oracle Construct."
- **Announcing**: "Attention. Deployment complete."
