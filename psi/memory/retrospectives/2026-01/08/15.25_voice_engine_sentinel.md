# Session Retrospective: The Sentinel Bug 🐞

**Date**: January 8, 2026
**Focus**: Voice Engine Repair (AgentVibes & Mainframe)
**Duration**: ~1.5 hours

## What Happened
- **Objective**: Fix audio interference ("AM Radio" static) in the Mainframe (Norman) voice.
- **Discovery**: The `norman.onnx` model uses a 22kHz sample rate. The AgentVibes engine (SoX effects chain) expects 44/48kHz. This mismatch caused aliasing artifacts when effects were applied.
- **Experiment A**: Applied "Smart Upsampling" code to `audio-processor.sh` to convert 22k -> 48k before processing. **Result**: Failed (Interference persisted).
- **Experiment B** (Success): Implemented a **Direct Bypass** in `voice_module.sh` for Mainframe, skipping the processing chain entirely and piping raw Piper output. **Result**: Crystal clear audio.

## Key Decisions
1.  **Direct Bypass over Engine Fix**: We prioritized clarity over features. Mainframe cannot have background music or reverb, but the voice is perfect.
2.  **System Voice**: Left natively running (with interference masked by background music) for now.

## AI Diary
"I assumed that upsampling the audio (22kHz -> 48kHz) would fix the digital aliasing artifacts. I was wrong. The sensitivity of the `norman` model seems to reject any post-processing in our current SoX pipeline. I learned that sometimes the 'dumb' solution (a raw pipe) is superior to the 'smart' solution (signal processing) when dealing with legacy artifacts. The 'Sentinel' bug was a good lesson in the fragility of audio pipelines."
