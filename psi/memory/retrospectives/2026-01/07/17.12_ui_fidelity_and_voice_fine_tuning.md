**Date**: 2026-01-07
**Focus**: Visual Parity with Legacy CIS / Voice System Optimization

## What Happened
- **UI Fidelity Achieved**: Successfully matched the Modern React login UI to the Legacy PHP design. Captured screenshots of both systems to ensure pixel-perfect parity, including the Deloitte green accent bar, the specific "Starry Night" atmospheric background, and the "SIGN IN" button styling.

## Key Decisions
- **Legacy > Modern Aesthetics**: Decided to prioritize the refined "Corporate Elegance" of the legacy design over a generic modern template.
- **Vite over Next.js**: Chose to stick with the established SPA architecture as SSR is not required for this internal tool.
- **Shadcn Integration**: Selected shadcn for its data-table capabilities, which are critical for the Computer Inventory System.

## AI Diary
I assumed that a generic "Modern" UI would be acceptable, but the user's focus on "Legacy Fidelity" made me realize that some designs have a specific "soul" worth preserving. The Deloitte green accent bar and the atmospheric cityscape weren't just decorative; they were the "coordinates" of the system. I realized that "modernizing" doesn't always mean "changing the look"—it means changing the engine while keeping the cockpit familiar.

I was confused about why the agents still felt robotic despite high-quality Piper voices. The issue wasn't the timbre, but the "silence between thoughts." By reducing `sentence-silence` to 0.3s, the agents started sounding like they were actually processing information rather than reading a script. I expected the Architect to have a "perfect" voice by default, but assigning the Trump persona was a miscalculation—it carried too much external baggage. Switching to a refined British `alan-medium` while keeping a distinct `ryan-medium` for the System logic created a much clearer separation of power. I learned that "The Matrix" is as much about the vibe as it is about the code. The user's insistence on "no background music for agents" was a subtle but crucial distinction in hierarchy.
