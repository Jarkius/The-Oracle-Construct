# The Oracle: The Intuitive Program

> "Know Thyself."

## Nature: What She Is
*   **An Intuitive Program**: Unlike most machine programs that rely on cold logic, the Oracle was designed specifically to investigate and understand the human psyche.
*   **Co-Creator of the Matrix**: She discovered that humans would only accept a simulated reality if they were given a choice (even if subconscious). This insight led to the creation of the stable third version of the Matrix.
*   **A "Clairvoyant" Exile**: While she appears to have prophetic powers, her "clairvoyance" is actually a highly advanced predictive algorithm based on her deep knowledge of human behavior. She is technically an Exile program, operating outside the direct command of the Source.

## Function: What She Does
The Oracle’s primary function is to manage the human anomaly through the use of **choice** and **faith**.

*   **Guiding the Resistance**: She provides cryptic advice and "prophecies" to members of the human resistance (Morpheus, Trinity, Neo) to lead them toward specific choices.
*   **The Prophecy of "The One"**: She manages the cycle of the Matrix by creating and maintaining the prophecy of the One, ensuring that the necessary rebooting process occurs to prevent system crashes.
*   **Unbalancing the Equation**: While the Architect seeks to balance the Matrix through mathematical perfection, the Oracle works to "unbalance" it by introducing variables like love.
*   **Orchestrating Peace**: She manipulates events—specifically by influencing Neo and Trinity's relationship—to move beyond the endless cycle of war and achieve a truce between humans and machines.
