## Query

How does YouTube recommendation algorithm work

## Response

In 2026, the YouTube recommendation algorithm is less like a static formula and more like a personal assistant that tries to predict your "future self."

Contrary to the popular myth, the algorithm doesn't "push" videos to people. Instead, it "pulls" videos for each specific user based on three distinct systems: Home, Suggested, and Search.

1. How It Decides What You See

The algorithm essentially follows a three-step process for every individual user:

Prediction: It looks at your watch history, what you’ve liked, and what similar viewers watch.

Selection: Out of billions of videos, it narrows down a "candidate pool" of a few thousand that match your current interests.
