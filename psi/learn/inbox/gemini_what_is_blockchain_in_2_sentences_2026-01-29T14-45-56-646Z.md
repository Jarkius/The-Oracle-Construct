## Response

A blockchain is a shared, digital ledger that records transactions across a network of computers in a way that makes the data nearly impossible to change or hack. It functions as a decentralized database where information is bundled into "blocks" and cryptographically linked together to create a permanent, transparent history.

Why?

The primary goal of blockchain is to establish trust without a central authority. By distributing the record-keeping across thousands of participants, no single person or organization (like a bank or government) has total control over the data.
