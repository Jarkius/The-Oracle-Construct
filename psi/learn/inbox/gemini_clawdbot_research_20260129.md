## Overview of ClawdBot

ClawdBot (or Claudebot) is an open-source, 24/7 AI agent that acts as a full-time "employee." It operates locally on your machine, allowing it to control your computer, access files, and perform tasks autonomously.

## Implementation Strategies

### 1. Personalization (First Step)
Treat like human employee - do a "brain dump" of interests, duties, tools [[16:54](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qkqe-uRhQJE&t=1014)]

### 2. Morning Brief
Daily summary: news, weather, proactive task list [[18:15](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qkqe-uRhQJE&t=1095)]

### 3. Coding Agent
Give GitHub access - reviews code, fixes bugs overnight [[19:08](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qkqe-uRhQJE&t=1148)]

### 4. Dedicated Email
Separate Gmail account - CC/forward for scheduling [[22:13](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qkqe-uRhQJE&t=1333)]

### 5. Second Brain
Auto-categorize ideas into research, tweets, app concepts [[22:54](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qkqe-uRhQJE&t=1374)]
