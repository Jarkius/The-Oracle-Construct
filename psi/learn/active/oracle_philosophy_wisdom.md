## Part 1: The Three Core Principles

### 1. Nothing is Deleted
> "History is not baggage. History is wealth."

- Archive rather than erase
- Timestamp everything
- Each past decision contains valuable insights
- Deletion causes loss of context and reasoning

**For Matrix**: Already in CLAUDE.md. Reinforce as Prime Directive #1.

### 2. Patterns Over Intentions
> Watch what someone does, not what they say they'll do.

- Observe actual behavior, not stated goals
- Humans self-deceive about values vs. priorities
- Tracking real patterns reveals authentic values
- This is authentic respect

**For Matrix**: Track what works across sessions, not what we planned.

### 3. External Brain, Not Command
> The AI reflects and remembers, never decides or directs.

- Mirror approach preserves human agency
- Shows context and history
- Leaves decisions to the human
- Commanding AI creates dependency

**For Matrix**: Oracle guides, never commands. The Operator decides.

```

    ↓ Many → Few

    ↓ Human Gate
```

## Part 4: Multi-Agent Patterns

### Hierarchical Capability Model
- **Haiku**: Fast, cheap work (gathering)
- **Opus**: Synthesis and complex judgment
- Pattern: "Haiku gathers, Opus synthesizes"

### Coordination Strategies
- **Write conflicts**: Unique output files per agent, merge centrally
- **Context gaps**: Non-coordinating tasks; orchestrator connects
- **Format issues**: Explicit formatting instructions to all agents

### Delegation Reframe
> Not "Can AI do this?" but "How many AI instances should work on this?"

### Cost-Benefit
Parallel: 7x faster, 67% cheaper, quality maintained.

## Part 5: Voice Philosophy

### Core Insight
> "Voice for moments. Text for information."

### Strategic Application
**Voice works for**:
- Social moments (greetings, announcements)
- Memory reinforcement
- Attention capture
- Bridging uncanny valley

**Text works for**:
- Technical precision
- Content requiring review
- Extended information
- Individual work contexts

### Speed Guidelines
- 1.5x speed (rate 300) = sweet spot
- Normal (200) = sluggish
- Double (400) = unintelligible

### Presence Over Efficiency
> "Engineering speed is not human listening speed."
